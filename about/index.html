Dr Luna Filipovic

Dr Luna Filipovic is a linguist specialising in forensic and legal language analysis, multilingual communication and investigative interviewing. She holds a PhD from the University of Cambridge and she has held multiple permanent and visiting positions at top academic institutions in the UK, the US and Italy. She has spent 20+ years combining academic research on the psychology of language, linguistic diversity and intercultural communication with professional practice in a variety of legal sectors such as law enforcement and jury trials.

Her work includes training and advising police forces in the UK, Europe and USA, alongside research and consultancy across legal, forensic and other professional contexts such as white-collar crime investigations and mental health assessment.

CV and detailed endorsements of Dr. Filipovic's work by the UK & US police and attorney clients are available upon request. Please send your requests using the contact tab.
